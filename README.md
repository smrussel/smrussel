## 📌 About Me

Hi! I'm **S M Russel**, a Software Developer and AI Systems Engineer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I specialize in bringing ideas to life with intelligent automation and seamless digital experiences using **Autonomous AI Agents**, **Agentic Workflows**, **Python**, and **Modern Full-Stack Architectures**.

- 🤖 **AI Agents & Speech-to-Speech Telephony**: Architecting autonomous agents, real-time voice receptionists (Pipecat, Twilio, OpenAI Realtime), and multi-modal RAG systems.
- ⚡ **Workflow & Business Automation**: Building end-to-end automation pipelines with **n8n**, **Zapier**, and custom Python scrapers for lead intelligence, CRM flows, and SEO.
- 🚀 **Full-Stack SaaS Engineering**: Crafting resilient backends with **FastAPI**, **Django REST/Ninja**, and **Flask**, paired with performant frontends in **Next.js**, **React**, and **Astro**.
- 💼 Open for **full-time software engineering roles**, **high-impact contract work**, and **freelance consulting**.
